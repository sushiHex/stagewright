Method: every camera position (one per authored transform time sample) is tested against each renderable environment gprim's world AABB. A position inside an AABB is then classified against that mesh with ray parity (+Z, +X and -X rays): **material** means inside the solid, **opening** means enclosed by the shell above and on both sides, i.e. clear air inside the nozzle or collector duct, and **air** means clear air that only falls inside the box. 'In view' is a frustum-against-AABB test: the part's box enters the frustum, which says nothing about occlusion.
