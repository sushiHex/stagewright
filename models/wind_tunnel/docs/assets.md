# Assets and licences

Policy: third-party assets must be CC0 (Poly Haven), and each one gets a row here with its source URL
and licence.

## Third-party assets

None are incorporated in the shipped environment or presentation scene. Their textures and model
geometry are procedural. The separately cloned blueprint and its verification assets stay under
ignored `external/` and are not redistributed; private verification renders stay under `build/private/`.

## Reference geometry

The Ahmed body in the presentation root (`tools/build_present.py`, `AHMED`) is built from
published dimensions, not from a downloaded model.

- **Primary reference:** S. R. Ahmed, G. Ramm and G. Faltin, "Some Salient Features of the
  Time-Averaged Ground Vehicle Wake", SAE Technical Paper 840300, 1984. The paper itself was not
  read; it is paywalled.
- **Restated by:** the ERCOFTAC knowledge base, test case AC1-05
  (https://www.kbwiki.ercoftac.org/w/index.php/Description_AC1-05), and COMSOL's Ahmed body model
  (https://doc.comsol.com/6.1/doc/com.comsol.help.models.cfd.ahmed_body/ahmed_body.html), both read
  2026-10-26.

| Dimension | Value (mm) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Length, width, height | 1044, 389, 288 | ERCOFTAC and COMSOL agree |
| Nose rounding | radius 100 | ERCOFTAC: curvature centred 100 mm from the front and the other faces |
| Ground clearance | 50 | ERCOFTAC |
| Stilt diameter | 30 | ERCOFTAC |
| Slant length, 25 degree rear | 222 | Both state 222. ERCOFTAC's split (forebody 182 + centre 640 + rear 222 = 1044) reads it as AXIAL; COMSOL computes the slant base as 288 - 222 sin 25, i.e. ALONG the slant. The build uses along the slant. Unresolved without the paper |
| Stilt positions | 300 and 727 from the nose, +/-170 from the centreline | NOT sourced: chosen for the presentation, so the base plates land on bare turntable clear of the centre belt frame and the four wheel-drive belts (`check_stilts` in `tools/build_present.py`). The front pair moved from 202 when the wheel-drive belts were added |

The prop is shown at 4.3 times model scale (about 4.49 x 1.67 x 1.24 m) on its transform. The parting lines
(nose block joint at 100 mm, slant insert joint at the start of the slant) and the stilt base plates
are presentation details, not part of the reference geometry.

## Generated textures

Every texture in `models/wind_tunnel/assets/textures/` is generated by `tools/build_env.py` (`make_textures()`), using
Warp kernels and nvImageCodec image writes, and is rewritten on every build. They are original to this repository, contain no
third-party material, and fall under the repository's own licence. Each set has three maps:
`_color` (sRGB albedo), `_normal` (tangent-space normal, OpenGL convention) and `_orm` (linear
occlusion, roughness and metallic in R, G and B). All tile seamlessly: the noise is built in the
frequency domain, so it is periodic by construction. The spectral noise uses Warp's seeded random
generator; its noise patterns differ from the earlier numpy build. No material dataset was fetched.

| Set | Size (px) | Repeat (cm) | Used on | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `perf` | 1024 | 40 x 40 | plenum walls and ceiling panels | perforated acoustic sheet: staggered 1.6 cm holes at 4 cm pitch, charcoal satin paint, black fill |
| `honeycomb` | 1024 | 72 x 72.7 (one lattice period) | honeycomb face in the contraction | flat-top hex cells, 6 cm side (exaggerated from real cell sizes so it reads on camera): aluminium walls, cells darkening with depth |
| `concrete` | 2048 | 600 x 600 | hall floor slab | sealed concrete: mottling, grain, roughness variation, 1.2 cm saw-cut seams |
| `epoxy` | 2048 | 400 x 400 | test-section floor | light grey epoxy, smoother, 0.8 cm expansion seams |
| `turntable` | 1024 | 300 x 300 | turntable top | lighter epoxy, no seams |
| `belt` | 512 | 20 x 20 | rolling-road centre belt and the four wheel-drive belts | dark rubber with longitudinal grooves at 2 cm pitch |
