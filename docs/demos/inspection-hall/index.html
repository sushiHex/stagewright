Physics record

The roof, deck and connecting yoke are dynamic bodies. One drive moves the roof. Joints move the deck through the passive yoke.

The graph compares powered and motor-disabled recordings. Yellow shows motor angle. Cyan shows deck velocity from recorded centre-of-mass positions, with a 13-sample quadratic fit and one fixed display scale. Forces were not recorded.

The motor-disabled control stays closed. This is an unloaded rigid-body test. Load capacity and contact safety were not tested. See the model guide for the measured operating conditions.

Construction record

One clean construction trial used the shipped COURTYARD source and declared engine tools. The first candidate failed. The constructor revised it from its own execution feedback. The second candidate is shown here. Source changes and records remain separate from this display.

# Inspection hall
