Physics record

The three bodies are dynamic. One angular drive controls the orange yoke. The blue and neutral bodies move through the joint constraints.

The graph compares the original powered and motor-disabled recordings. The cyan arrow shows wall velocity derived from the recorded centre-of-mass path. It uses a local quadratic fit over 13 solver samples and one fixed display scale. It is a motion vector, not a force measurement. The yellow arc shows the recorded motor angle. Torque and joint reaction loads were not recorded.
